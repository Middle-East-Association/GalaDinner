An evening among the UK-Middle East business community

Held in the prestigious surroundings of One Great George Street, the evening offers a unique opportunity for organisations to connect with influential decision-makers from across the UK-Middle East business community. The event regularly attracts senior representatives from major corporations, embassies, investors, government officials, and leading policy experts.

Last year’s dinner welcomed 170 guests and was fully booked, featuring Shadow Foreign Secretary the Rt Hon Priti Patel MP as keynote speaker and James Landale as master of ceremonies for the charity raffle. For 2026, we expect over 220 guests.

We are delighted to announce that our keynote speaker is Sir Oliver Robbins KCMG CB, former Permanent Under-Secretary at the FCDO.
